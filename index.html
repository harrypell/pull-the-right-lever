Brighton & Hove school admissions

Ask our councillors to look at the evidence — it takes 60 seconds.

The council will decide this autumn how secondary admissions work from September 2028. Professor Adam Dennett (Professor of Urban Analytics, UCL) has independently analysed what's really driving school attainment in our city — and is presenting his findings at a free public lecture, “How to Pull the Right Lever”, on Monday 21 July, 7–9pm at BHASVIC (also joinable via Zoom). Invite the councillors who shape this policy to come and see the data before the next consultation is written.

Step 1

Your details

These are dropped into the letter automatically. Councillors give far more weight to letters from identifiable local families.

Step 2

Who it goes to

Pre-selected: the four councillors with most influence over admissions policy. Pick your ward in Step 1 to add your own local councillors too.

Step 3

Your letter

Send it as it is, or make it yours — personal letters carry the most weight. Your details from Step 1 appear as you type.
